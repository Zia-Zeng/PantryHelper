# Pantry Helper Mobile · iPhone 15 Pro Max

English mobile interaction prototype based on the Pantry Helper interaction specification. Target viewport: **430 × 932**.

## Experience
- First-time setup for goals, people, allergies and starter foods.
- Four primary tabs: Today, Shop, Pantry and Recipes.
- Expiry review with explicit food-safety limits.
- Explainable shopping quantities, shopping mode and user-confirmed stock-in.
- Receipt recognition demo with mandatory confirmation and missing-unit validation.
- Manual pantry entry, search, status filters and amount calibration.
- Recipe matching, missing-ingredient shopping and actual-use confirmation after cooking.
- Pantry Plus weekly-plan preview with pantry-deducted shopping gaps.

## Prototype boundaries
- All data is local to the browser. There is no server, user account, real sharing or notification service.
- Receipt recognition uses clearly labelled sample results. Uploaded images are not sent to OCR or AI.
- Prices and nutrition are estimates. The Plus preview starts no subscription or payment.
- Dates support planning but never determine whether food is safe or spoiled.
- Allergy and special-diet guidance does not replace checking labels or professional medical advice.

## Visual direction
The interface follows the supplied mobile references: generous white space, high-contrast editorial typography, warm orange accents, food photography, quiet dividers, iOS safe areas and rounded bottom sheets. It does not copy any referenced brand or screen verbatim.
